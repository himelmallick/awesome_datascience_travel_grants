# Awesome Data Science Travel Grants

A curated, community-maintained list of student paper awards, travel grants and early-career awards in statistics, biostatistics, data science and computational biology.

Every entry links to the official page and says who can apply, what the award covers, and when the call usually closes. The deadlines are general: the usual date or time of year, which holds from one year to the next. The exact day can shift, so confirm it on the official page before you plan around it.

Spotted something out of date, or know an award that is missing? [Tell us with a short form](https://github.com/himelmallick/awesome_datascience_travel_grants/issues/new/choose). No Git knowledge is needed.

**74 awards. Last verified against the official pages on October 3, 2026.**

## Contributing

There are three ways to help, from quickest to most hands-on:

1. **Fill in a form.** [Suggest an award](https://github.com/himelmallick/awesome_datascience_travel_grants/issues/new?template=new-award.yml) or [report a correction](https://github.com/himelmallick/awesome_datascience_travel_grants/issues/new?template=correction.yml). A maintainer adds it for you.
2. **Edit the table in your browser.** Open [`awards.csv`](awards.csv), click the pencil icon, change or add a row, and propose the change. GitHub walks you through the rest.
3. **Open a pull request** the usual way.

Please edit `awards.csv` only. This README is rebuilt from it automatically. The full guide, including what belongs on the list, is in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
